Squidpal - alpha
================

Squidpal is a small menu-bar app that keeps a diary of your workday. It reads
what's on screen in the apps you use and what you type, and saves it as text
files (one per day) in a folder you choose, so your AI assistant can help you
keep track of follow-ups and context.

IMPORTANT BEFORE YOU START
- It saves almost everything you see and type: email, chats, web pages,
  documents - including what other people write to you.
- Everything stays on your computer, in the folder you choose. The app makes no
  network requests. If you pick a synced folder (iCloud, Drive, Dropbox), the
  files go there.
- Passwords, API keys, card numbers, national ID numbers and one-time codes are
  masked before anything is saved, and password fields and password managers are
  never read. Masking is pattern-based and not a guarantee.
- The menu-bar icon always shows whether it's recording (coloured) or paused
  (grey). Pause from the menu when you're doing something you don't want logged.

INSTALL
1. Open the DMG and drag Squidpal to the Applications shortcut.
2. Double-click it in Applications. macOS will say it can't be opened because it
   isn't from a registered developer. Click Done.
3. Open System Settings -> Privacy & Security, scroll to the message about the
   app, and click "Open Anyway". Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
   (Terminal alternative: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Squidpal.app)

FIRST RUN
1. macOS asks for Accessibility access. Open System Settings -> Privacy &
   Security -> Accessibility and turn it on.
2. Choose a folder for the day files.
3. The menu-bar icon turns coloured = recording. Done.

Browsers may ask whether the app can control them; that's only used to read the
current page's address. You can decline.

IN THE MENU
- Pause: 15 minutes, 1 hour, or until you turn it back on.
- Open today's file, show the folder, delete today's file, change folder.
- Number of records, number of redactions, and file size today.

NEW VERSION
Quit from the menu and replace the app in Applications. macOS may stop trusting
the new version; if so, remove the app from the Accessibility list (minus
button), start it, and turn it on again.

UNINSTALL
Quit from the menu, move the app to the Trash, and remove it from the
Accessibility list. The day files stay in your folder until you delete them.

BUG REPORTS
Say what you did and what happened. Don't send the day files without reading
through them first.
